# Visa Sponsorship in Early-Career Job Ads, 9 Countries, November 2026

[![License: CC BY 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC_BY_4.0-lightgrey.svg)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) [![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.24520890.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24520890)

Aggregate counts from live job ads collected by [Qarera](https://www.qarera.com), ads open and seen **November 10 – 24, 2026**.

- 📊 **Full report:** [www.qarera.com/reports/graduate-jobs-visa-sponsorship-2026](https://www.qarera.com/reports/graduate-jobs-visa-sponsorship-2026)
- 📄 **License:** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) — free to use with attribution to Qarera.

## Key findings

- Internship, graduate and junior ads in 9 countries: **30 offered visa sponsorship, 1,527 refused it** (about 51 refusals per offer, against about 9 for senior ads).
- Only **11 employers** offered sponsorship in an early-career ad (each read by hand).
- UK: 4 early-career offers, 304 refusals.
- UK: **404 of 1123 live graduate and junior ads (36%)** are at **172 employers** on the Home Office register of licensed sponsors (Nov 23, 2026).

## Files

| File | Rows | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `sponsorship-by-country-and-level.csv` | 9 countries × 4 levels | Ads explicitly offering and explicitly refusing visa sponsorship, by level in the job title. |
| `early-career-employers-offering-sponsorship.csv` | 11 | Every employer whose early-career ad offered sponsorship, read by hand. |
| `uk-licensed-sponsors-with-graduate-jobs.csv` | 172 | UK licensed sponsors (Skilled Worker / Global Business Mobility) with live graduate or junior ads. |

Counts are ads, one per job (the same job on a career site and on LinkedIn/Indeed counts once). Percentages are shares of
the ads in that row. No ad text and no personal data are included.

## Methodology and limits

Every open ad seen November 10 – 24, 2026 in the UK, Ireland, the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Germany, the Netherlands and Singapore that mentions a visa, sponsorship or relocation (44,225 ads in English, German, Dutch or French), labelled with the phrase rules of Qarera's visa sponsorship report (blind check 40/40 refusals, 36/40 offers). Every early-career offer was read by hand and three misread employers removed. Levels come from job titles; 'associate' is not counted as junior. UK graduate and junior ads (internships, placements, apprenticeships left out) were matched to the GOV.UK register of licensed sponsors of 2026-11-23 by reduced organisation name, generic names and agencies skipped.
